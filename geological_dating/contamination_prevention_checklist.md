## II. Dynamic Mechanical Boring Protocols
* [ ] **Nitrogen Envelope Deployment:** Pressurize the micro-boring extraction assembly with a continuous positive-pressure stream of high-purity (99.999% ultra-pure grade) Nitrogen ($N_2$) gas to sweep ambient atmospheric particulates and modern organic spores away from the drill path.
* [ ] **Tooling Material Non-Contamination:** Utilize diamond-tipped core bits matrixed exclusively within copper-alloy shanks. Avoid the deployment of steel tools coated in carbon-bearing industrial cutting oils or oil-slicked cooling fluids.
* [ ] **Dynamic Temperature Interlocking:** Monitor extraction core face temperature. Frictional heat must be kept below 65°C using compressed gas cooling lines to prevent recrystallization or outgassing of trapped noble fluid inclusions within the quartz lattice.

## III. Core Extraction and Sealed Containment Logs

* [ ] **Dark Transition Execution:** Snap the core sample specimen directly from the interior backing face under the nitrogen shroud and slide it immediately into the containment sleeve without exposing the internal surfaces to ambient light arrays.
