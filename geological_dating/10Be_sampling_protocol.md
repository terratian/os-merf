# Protocol for Cosmogenic Nuclide Joint Sampling (¹⁰Be / ²⁶Al)
# Copyright (C) 2026 Joshua Sultan | GNU GPL v3

1. **Joint Isolation:** Identify tightly sealed joints showing no signs of modern shifted environmental exposure.
2. **Micro-Bore Execution:** Utilize a diamond-tipped micro-core bore under a high-purity nitrogen envelope to prevent wind-borne organic contamination.
3. **Isotope Target:** Isolate Beryllium-10 via Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS) to date the stone cut directly.
