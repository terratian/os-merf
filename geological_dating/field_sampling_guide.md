## I. Mineralogical Specimen Criteria
To ensure high-precision validation via Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS), target interlocking stones must satisfy strict mineralogical baselines:
* **Target Phenocrysts:** Seek out igneous matrices (granite, granodiorite, rhyolite, or andesite) with a minimum quartz (\(SiO_2\)) volume fraction of 15–20%. Crystalline quartz is the required target lattice for retaining cosmogenic Beryllium-10 ($^{10}\text{Be}$) and Aluminium-26 ($^{26}\text{Al}$).
* **Grain Size Thresholds:** Prioritize coarse-grained textures (phaneritic phenocrysts > 2mm). Larger, intact crystal boundaries provide superior mechanical stability against structural cracking during the clean acid-leaching separation phase.
* **Alteration Exclusions:** Avoid sampling stone blocks exhibiting advanced chemical weathering, chlorite alteration, or localized surface kaolinization, as compromised mineral lattices lead to accelerated isotope leaching.

## II. Geometry of the Contact Interface
The target bore path must be planned relative to the three-dimensional geometry of the interlocking structural system:
1. **Depth Selection:** The micro-bore core must drill down at least 10–15 centimeters into the internal interface seam to bypass the superficial zone affected by ambient cosmic rays or micro-fracture exposure.
2. **Perpendicular Vectoring:** Orient the diamond-tipped core tool at an absolute 90-degree angle (normal vector) relative to the internal hidden contact plane. Misaligned entry vectors cause core shear failure and introduce external dust particulates.
3. **Structural Load Guards:** Never extract core samples from high-stress structural stress points, structural load bearing lintel corners, or critical lower geometric keys. Focus sampling entirely on non-load bearing interior stabilizing puzzle seams.

## III. Verification Data Collection Log
Prior to physical extraction, the field team must commit the following field notes to the local database file:
* **Digital Azimuth Matrix:** Record absolute structural orientation using a digital transit compass mapped to actual true grid north.
* **Geometric Inclination:** Document the slope inclination angle of both the outer wall face and the inner joint seam surface.
* **Topographical Shielding Profile:** Capture a complete 360-degree LiDAR or photogrammetric horizon scan from the site coordinates. This calculates localized cosmic ray shielding coefficients caused by nearby mountain ridges or valley geometry.
