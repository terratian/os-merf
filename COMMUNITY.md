This document establishes the interaction protocols for independent researchers, adjunct non-tenured lecturers, and tenured university faculty operating outside standard institutional funding loops. We utilize decentralized science (DeSci) infrastructure to ensure all telemetry, models, and analytical tools remain un-censorable and completely transparent.

## I. Interaction Framework: DeSci Publish
To protect data integrity and bypass traditional journal paywalls/gatekeeping, all major formal text entries, field manuals, and datasets must be anchored via **DeSci Publish**:
* **Preprint Archiving:** Submit manuscripts and raw field records directly to DeSci Publish. Every upload automatically generates an unalterable digital object identifier (DOI) backed by IPFS decentralized storage nodes.
* **Open Repository Syncing:** When submitting point clouds (.e57) or codebase changes, link your GitHub repository directly to your DeSci Publish profile to lock code and data together.
* **Anonymity Options:** Independent researchers or adjunct faculty members who risk losing institutional access or funding may publish under verified cryptographic pseudonyms to protect their professional security.

## II. Interaction Framework: ResearchHub
For post-publication peer review, code audits, and rapid feedback loops, the OS-MERF ecosystem maps directly to **ResearchHub**:
* **Open Discussion and Auditing:** Every mathematical model (such as `metabolic_model.py`) and algorithmic line trace must be posted to relevant ResearchHub channels for public verification.
* **Micro-Grants & Bounties:** Independent researchers can post specific computational tasks, field dataset requirements, or laboratory isotope sample runs as open bounties, funded via community-driven decentralized tokens.
* **Cross-Disciplinary Reviews:** Adjunct faculty from diverse departments (Structural Engineering, Geomorphology, Evolutionary Biology) use this space to debate cross-domain anomalies without traditional domain silo enforcement.

## III. Execution Code of Conduct
1. **Reproducibility Mandate:** Every claim regarding a geomechanical or biological anomaly must link directly to an open dataset, a functional code module, or an verifiable laboratory checklist protocol.
2. **Copyleft Adherence:** Any researcher pulling data or script architectures from this workspace must explicitly maintain attribution to Joshua Sultan (terratian) and release all derivative platforms under identical GPL v3 parameters.
